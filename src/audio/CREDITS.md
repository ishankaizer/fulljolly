# Where the bundled sounds come from

These are the starting voice of the app. A recording made in the sound library always replaces them.

| Sounds | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The 26 letter sounds (`a` to `z`) and `qu` | [Open Letter Sounds](https://github.com/tavinathanson/nemophones) by Purple Computer, voice "marin" (computer-made, each clip listened to and approved by a person there) | CC0 1.0, no credit needed |
| `sh`, `ch` | Cut from the Wikimedia Commons recordings "Voiceless postalveolar fricative" and "Voiceless postalveolar affricate" by Peter Isotalo (the vowels around the consonant were removed) | CC BY-SA 3.0 |

Notes for the teacher:

- The letter sounds are US English. The short `o` is the American one (as in "hot" said the American way). Re-record it
  in the sound library if you want the British or Indian sound.
- `th`, `ng` and the long vowel sounds (`ai`, `ee`, `oa`, `oo`, `ar`, `or`, `er`, `ow`, `oi`, `ou`, `ay`, `ea`, `igh`)
  have no bundled recording yet. The device's voice says them until they are recorded.
- Every clip was checked by machine before it was added (length, hiss or voice, loudness); nobody on this project has
  listened to them yet.
